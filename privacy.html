PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle the information you share through this website.

Rogue Global Solutions ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information you send us

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, and message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to prepare a proposal for you.

Contact form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service.

What we don't do

  • We do not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you've sent us at any time by emailing contact.rogueglobalsolutions@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website changes. The latest version will always be on this page.
